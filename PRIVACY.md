# Privacy policy

Casement is a Chrome extension that previews a web page at another screen size. This policy describes the only data it handles.

## Data stored on your computer

Casement saves these settings in Chrome local storage on your own computer:

- favorite device ids
- the last eight devices you opened
- the last device you selected

That data is not sent to the developer, and Casement has no account system, analytics, or server of its own.

## Pages you open

When you click the Casement icon or press Alt+Shift+C, the extension reads the address of the current tab so it can show that page in the preview. The address stays in the preview window on your computer.

If a site refuses to be shown in a frame, **Allow this site** asks Chrome for permission for that site only. Casement then removes frame-blocking response headers for the preview. It does not read the page contents, passwords, or form data.

## What Casement does not do

- It does not sell or share data.
- It does not use data for advertising or credit decisions.
- It does not collect personal information for an account.
